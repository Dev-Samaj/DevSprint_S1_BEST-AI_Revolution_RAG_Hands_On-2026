# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Dev Samaj is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone participating in our workshops and community.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants should:

- Respect other participants.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect different skill levels.
- Avoid harassment or discrimination.
- Give credit when using existing work.
- Keep discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following are not allowed:

- Harassment
- Hate speech
- Personal attacks
- Plagiarism
- Sharing private information
- Malicious code
- Deliberate disruption of other participants' work

## Reporting

If you experience or observe inappropriate behaviour, contact the Dev Samaj organizing team.

We want everyone to learn, build and collaborate.
